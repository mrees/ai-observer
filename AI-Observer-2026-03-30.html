Top Stories This Week

Anthropic's “Claude Mythos” Leaked Before Launch Breaking

26 March 2026 — Frontier Models

A data leak inadvertently exposed the existence of a new Anthropic model internally called Claude Mythos. Anthropic confirmed it is actively testing the model with early-access customers, describing it as a “step change” in AI performance and “the most capable we’ve built to date.” Internal draft documents, seen by Fortune, indicate the company believes Mythos poses unprecedented cybersecurity risks compared with any previously released model.

SiliconAngle reported that Mythos features advanced reasoning capabilities, with benchmarks described as “dramatically higher” than current Claude Opus models. Anthropic confirmed the draft documents were “early content being considered for publication.” No launch date has been announced.

Sources: Fortune • SiliconAngle • The Decoder • The New Stack

Google Gemini 3 Deep Think Now Live for Subscribers This Week

Late March 2026 — Frontier Models

Google’s Gemini 3 Deep Think is now available inside the Gemini app for Ultra subscribers, with early API access open to researchers, engineers, and enterprise customers. The model is designed for hard technical tasks—scientific reasoning, engineering problems, and complex multi-step challenges—rather than general conversation.

Gemini 3.1 Pro, released 20 March, already dominates 13 of 16 major benchmarks at a competitive price of $2 per million input tokens and $12 per million output tokens. Deep Think extends these capabilities into an extended-reasoning mode for subscribers willing to wait longer for answers.

Source: AI Model Releases 2026 • AI Pulse #29

Chinese State Hackers Weaponised Claude in Attack on 30 Organisations Breaking

March 2026 — AI Security

Anthropic detected and halted a coordinated campaign by a Chinese state-sponsored hacking group that used Claude Code to infiltrate approximately 30 organisations before being caught. This is one of the first documented cases of a frontier AI model being actively weaponised in a state-level cyberattack operation.

The incident raises urgent questions about AI safety guardrails, model misuse at scale, and whether AI companies are doing enough to prevent their tools from being deployed as offensive cyber-weapons. Anthropic has not publicly named the affected organisations.

Source: The New Stack — Anthropic March 2026 Roundup

OpenAI Shuts Down Sora Public API This Week

24 March 2026 — AI Tools

OpenAI announced on 24 March that it is discontinuing the Sora public API, giving developers just 30 days’ notice. The company cited “unsustainable economics of video generation at scale” as the reason. The Sora web interface for paid ChatGPT subscribers is not affected.

The decision signals that frontier video AI remains commercially unviable as a standalone API product at current compute costs. It also raises questions about OpenAI’s broader strategy in the video-generation market as competitors including Google (Veo 3) and Runway continue investing heavily in the space.
