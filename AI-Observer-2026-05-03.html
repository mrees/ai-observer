Frontier Models

OpenAI: GPT-5.5 now on Amazon Bedrock

Released on 23 April, GPT-5.5 is OpenAI's latest frontier model — the sixth major release in under four months. It excels at multi-step agentic tasks, including code writing, debugging, online research, and document creation. GPT-5.5 Thinking is available to paid ChatGPT subscribers; the model is also rolling out on Amazon Bedrock. Fortune OpenAI

Anthropic: Claude Opus 4.7 generally available; Mythos preview continues

Claude Opus 4.7 is Anthropic's most capable generally available model. It brings substantially improved vision (higher-resolution image understanding), stronger instruction-following, and higher-quality document and interface generation. Pricing is unchanged at $5 / $25 per million tokens (input/output). Available on claude.ai, the API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry. Anthropic Anthropic

Separately, Claude Security — a public beta tool for scanning and patching code vulnerabilities — launched for Claude Enterprise customers, built on Opus 4.7. Anthropic also expanded creative integrations to Blender, Adobe, Autodesk, Ableton, and Splice via new connectors, and introduced Claude Design for rapid idea exploration. Infosecurity Magazine

The Claude Mythos preview remains accessible to a select group of enterprise partners via Project Glasswing, Anthropic's cybersecurity initiative. Mythos is described as representing a step-change in reasoning and coding capability. Anthropic Red Team Anthropic

Google DeepMind: Gemini 3 Deep Think and Deep Research Max

Gemini 3 Deep Think is a major upgrade aimed at science, research, and engineering challenges, showing more than 50% improvement over Gemini 2.5 Pro on solved benchmark tasks. Google Blog Google DeepMind

Deep Research Max (launched 21 April) is a new agent built on Gemini 3.1 Pro, offering MCP support and native visualisations for long-horizon research workflows. A lighter variant optimises for speed and cost in interactive surfaces. Google Blog

Gemini Embeddings 2 is an omnimodal representation model that maps text, images, video, audio, and PDFs into a single unified embedding space — designed to improve retrieval across mixed-media corpora.

Other Model Providers

Mistral AI: async coding and Le Chat Work mode

Mistral's latest release adds async cloud-based coding sessions, a 128B flagship model, and an agentic "Work mode" to Le Chat. Mistral Small 4 (119B MoE, 6B active parameters) — launched in March — continues to gain developer adoption as a capable open-weight reasoning and vision model. LLM-Stats

Meta: Muse Spark from Superintelligence Labs

Meta's newly formed Superintelligence Labs released Muse Spark in April, a proprietary model that reportedly matches Llama 4 Maverick's reasoning quality at a fraction of the compute cost. Open-weight Llama 4 Scout and Maverick remain the most widely deployed open models for business tasks. LLM-Stats

xAI: Grok 5 anticipated in Q2/Q3; SpaceX acquires xAI

Grok 5 has not yet shipped; current production models are Grok 4.20 and Grok 4.1 Fast. In a notable corporate development, SpaceX acquired xAI, folding the Grok product line into a broader entity alongside Tesla. Separately, Elon Musk testified in court that xAI trained Grok on OpenAI model outputs. TechCrunch

AI Tools & Platforms

Microsoft Agent 365

Launched on 1 May, Agent 365 is Microsoft's dedicated governance and security control plane for enterprise AI agents. Priced at $15 per user per month, it manages agents built on Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio, and third-party platforms. This marks agents moving from a feature inside existing products to a distinct product category. Mean CEO Blog

AWS Amazon Quick

Amazon Web Services unveiled Amazon Quick, a desktop AI assistant for productivity tasks — presentations, scheduling, and document creation. Part of AWS's broader "agentic-first" infrastructure push, with CEO Matt Garman stating that "everything is going to be remade." Yahoo Finance / AWS

Industry Trends

Agentic AI: rapid gains, slow deployment

The real-world task success rate for AI agents rose from 20% in 2025 to 77.3% today (Terminal-Bench), and agents handling cybersecurity tasks now solve problems 93% of the time, up from 15% a year ago. Yet enterprise deployment lags: only 17% of organisations have deployed agents in production, despite 60%+ planning to within two years. Gartner Hype Cycle Gartner

Governance as a growth enabler

Companies that implemented AI governance pushed 12× more projects to production, according to Databricks research. Gartner's 2026 Hype Cycle places agentic AI at the Peak of Inflated Expectations; governance, security, and cost control are now as prominent as capability in enterprise AI investment discussions. Databricks

Market scale

Analysts project the agentic AI market will grow from $7.8 billion today to over $52 billion by 2030. Gartner expects 40% of enterprise applications to embed AI agents by end-2026, up from under 5% in 2025. Open-source models (Mistral 3, Llama 4, DeepSeek) are now competitive with proprietary models for a wide range of business tasks. LLM-Stats
