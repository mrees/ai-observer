2. FDA Eases Oversight on AI-Enabled Wearables and Clinical Decision Software

Source: STAT News  /  National Law Review
What happened

In January 2026 the FDA updated two guidance documents — on General Wellness Devices and Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Software — substantially relaxing regulatory requirements for consumer wearables that report physiological metrics (blood pressure, SpO2, glucose signals) and for AI tools that provide a single, clinically appropriate recommendation. Devices intended solely for general wellness with non-diagnostic outputs can now avoid FDA device classification entirely.

This is a green light for digital health studies in the 75+ population. Second-order effect

Non-profits that establish microbiome measurement as a standard outcome in their nutrition trials now will be well positioned as this becomes a regulatory endpoint expectation.
